## Notes from the intake call (as the account manager would type them)

Brannock & Daughter is a joinery workshop in Thomastown, County Kilkenny.
Seán Brannock opened the workshop in 1987. His daughter Aoife has run it since 2019.
There are three joiners and an apprentice.

What they make:
- Staircases. Made to measure for new builds and old houses, in solid timber.
- Kitchens and fitted furniture. Kitchens, wardrobes and shelving, built in the workshop and fitted on site.
- Sash windows. Repair and replacement for older houses, matched to the windows that are there.

Timber: Irish oak, ash, and reclaimed pitch pine. Every board is chosen in the yard.
"We don't use veneered board for anything you can see."

How a job runs:
1. Site visit. We come to the house, measure and listen. Free within 40 km of Thomastown.
2. Drawing and quote. A drawing and a fixed price within two weeks of the visit.
3. Making. Everything is made in our workshop in Thomastown.
4. Fitting. The people who made it fit it.

Lead time: we are currently booking work for 10 to 14 weeks from the date of the quote.

Contact: hello@brannock.example. Workshop visits by appointment, Monday to Friday.
