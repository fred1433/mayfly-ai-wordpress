About.
DIGITAL VISIONARY
Liam Dennehy, Managing Director at Mayfly Digital, possesses a rich 25-year legacy in advancing Irish brands through digital innovation. Liam’s methodology is characterised by its clarity and ease of application.
His strategies are straightforward yet impactful, offering a practical and rewarding framework for enhancing your brand’s presence, accelerating sales, and amplifying engagement across social platforms.
Central to Liam’s ethos is the belief that effective marketing melds elegant design with simplicity and value augmentation. Guided by this clear strategy, Liam has collaborated with over 300 brands, producing a myriad of compelling marketing messages and developing extensive digital campaigns and websites.
