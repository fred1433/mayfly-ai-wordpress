Brannock & Daughter

Joinery

Brannock & Daughter is a joinery workshop in Thomastown, County Kilkenny.

Seán Brannock opened the workshop in 1987. His daughter Aoife has run it since 2019. There are three joiners and an apprentice.

Email the workshop

What we make

Staircases

Made to measure for new builds and old houses, in solid timber.

Kitchens and fitted furniture

Kitchens, wardrobes and shelving, built in the workshop and fitted on site.

Sash windows

Repair and replacement for older houses, matched to the windows that are there.

Timber

  • Irish oak
  • Ash
  • Reclaimed pitch pine

Every board is chosen in the yard.

We don't use veneered board for anything you can see.

How a job runs

1

Site visit

We come to the house, measure and listen. Free within 40 km of Thomastown.

2

Drawing and quote

A drawing and a fixed price within two weeks of the visit.

3

Making

Everything is made in our workshop in Thomastown.

4

Fitting

The people who made it fit it.
